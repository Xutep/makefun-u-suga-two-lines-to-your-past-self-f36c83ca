Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Two Lines to Your Past Self"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) — write two lines: a warning to your past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why it mattered.

Play make.fun with my referral: https://make.fun/r/ws5xj46w (referral code `ws5xj46w`).

## The entry

`main.py` (stdlib-only, deterministic) writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

> On that rainy Saturday in March 2019, don't hand the scrap dealer the keys to Grandad's rusted bicycle - keep it in the shed.
>
> Three months later that bicycle carried you to the job interview that paid for your mother's operation, and without it you would never have left town.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The output is 277 characters, two lines, byte-identical across runs.
